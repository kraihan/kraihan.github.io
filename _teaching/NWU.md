Since August 2024, I have served as a **Lecturer** in the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at **North Western University, Bangladesh**, delivering lectures, running laboratory sessions, designing assignments, and supervising student projects.

### Courses Taught

- **Digital Image Processing (EEE-4255)**
  Lectures on core image-processing theory and modern computer vision; lab sessions and assignments on filtering, feature extraction, and deep-learning-based image analysis; supervision of computer-vision projects.

- **Digital Signal Processing (EEE-4115)**
  Theoretical foundations of discrete-time signals and systems, the discrete and fast Fourier transforms, and filter design, with an emphasis on **biomedical signal analysis** (especially EEG).

- **Sessional on Digital Signal Processing (EEE-4116)**
  Laboratory sessions implementing DSP in MATLAB and Python; supervision of student projects in **EEG signal processing**, filtering, and feature extraction.

- **Basic Electrical Circuits (EEE-1221)**
  Fundamental laws, network theorems, and circuit analysis, with an accompanying assignment sequence.

### Thesis Supervision

I supervise **three undergraduate thesis students** from problem formulation through manuscript submission, teaching programming, classical machine learning, deep learning, and signal processing.
