CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION
Under review · ICLR 2027

One step from noise to data.

PT-Flow: One-Step Schrödinger-Bridge Generation by Proximal Tilting

A Schrödinger-bridge formulation for single-evaluation generation, built around a proximal-tilting importance estimator.

My contribution. Proposed PT-Flow, developed the estimator, and ran ImageNet-1K experiments on Clemson’s Palmetto cluster.

CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION
Accepted · BMVC 2026

Making geometric learning practical.

Orthogonal polynomial approximation for matrix-log normalization in global covariance pooling

Polynomial approximations replace eigendecomposition in matrix-log normalization, enabling GPU-friendly covariance pooling.

Research focus. Efficient matrix functions that preserve the geometry of covariance representations.

arXiv ↗
CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION
Under review · IEEE TPAMI

Image restoration through transport.

AutoLumNet: Monotone optimal transport for single-shot exposure correction

AutoLumNet treats single-shot exposure correction as monotone optimal transport, addressing both under- and over-exposure.

My contribution. Designed the exposure-aware network and reformulated the correction step as a transport problem.

arXiv ↗
CONCEPTUAL ILLUSTRATION
Under review · AISTATS 2027

Let the representation keep its geometry.

Equivariant geodesic networks: end-to-end classification on the SPD manifold

Equivariant Geodesic Networks combine equivariant mapping, Riemannian pooling, and geodesic attention directly on the SPD manifold.

Open-source release. Authored egnlib, a Python package implementing the EGN architecture and a stable matrix-logarithm backward pass.
