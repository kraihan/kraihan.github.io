Efficient, geometry-aware deep learning

I work on generative models that need one network evaluation instead of hundreds, on image restoration posed as optimal transport rather than as a reconstruction loss, and on architectures that stay on the Riemannian manifold their features actually live on. Most of my applied work is in neural and biomedical signals, where covariance — an SPD matrix — is the natural representation.

Applying to PhD programs for the 2027 cycle in machine learning and electrical & computer engineering. I am looking for advisors working on generative modeling, optimal transport, and geometric deep learning.

Research

What I work on

  • One- and few-step generative modeling Closed-form flow and transport formulations that collapse iterative sampling into a single network evaluation, together with the stabilizers the degenerate-path regime needs in practice.
  • Restoration as optimal transport Low-light enhancement and exposure correction posed as a monotone transport map between degraded and well-exposed intensity distributions, instead of a heuristic reconstruction objective.
  • Geometry-aware learning on SPD manifolds Equivariant layers, geodesic operators, Riemannian pooling, and numerically stable matrix-function approximation — including GPU-friendly polynomial substitutes for the matrix logarithm.
  • Structured representations for neural signals EEG-based affective and cognitive state decoding, where spectral coherence and differential entropy give the covariance structure that geometric models operate on.

Research experience

Where the work was done

Independent researcher November 2024 — present
  • Derived a closed-form flow formulation that collapses iterative diffusion sampling into a single network evaluation, removing the multi-step solver conventional flow matching requires; implemented the full reference training codebase with stabilizers for the degenerate-path regime.
  • Proposed Equivariant Geodesic Networks, an end-to-end SPD-manifold classifier built from equivariant mapping, Riemannian pooling, geometric bias, and geodesic attention layers.
  • Derived orthogonal-polynomial approximations for matrix-logarithm normalization in global covariance pooling, cutting cost and enabling GPU execution of a previously CPU-bound operation.
  • Designed AutoLumNet, reformulating single-shot exposure correction as monotone optimal transport.
  • Developed DAFNet, pairing a local attention CNN with a global graph-attention transformer and covariance pooling for EEG emotion recognition.
Research collaborator — Dept. of EEE, KUET May 2024 — July 2024 · Supervisor: Dr. Md. Mahbub Hasan

Undergraduate thesis researcher — Dept. of EEE, KUET January 2023 — March 2024 · Advisor: Prof. Dr. Mohiuddin Ahmad
